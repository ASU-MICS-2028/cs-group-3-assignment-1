Why nonce reuse is dangerous: GCM encrypts by XOR with a keystream derived from key + nonce.
Reusing a nonce with the same key reuses the keystream, so XOR of two ciphertexts = XOR of the
two plaintexts, and it exposes the authentication key, letting an attacker forge valid tags.
